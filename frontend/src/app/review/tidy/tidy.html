Lightroom lets PhotoKeeper put a photo into an album, but only you can take one out again. So when a lot have piled up, the way round it is:

  1. Remove all photos from the Keeper album
  2. Restore the photos that belong there

Note: Lightroom's synching might take a while.

Check which photos you need to remove

These are sitting in an album they no longer belong in. Lightroom only offers remove from this album inside the album itself; a search can show just these photos, but there it can only delete them outright. So: open the album to take them out, or the search to delete them.
