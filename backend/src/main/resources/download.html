How to install

  1. 1 Download the APK above and open it from your notifications bar.
  2. 2 If prompted, go to Settings → Apps → Special app access → Install unknown apps and allow your browser.
  3. 3 Tap Install on the confirmation screen. PhotoKeeper will appear in your app drawer.
  4. 4 Connect to your home Wi-Fi — the app talks to your Raspberry Pi.
